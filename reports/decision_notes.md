# What I would investigate next

Late delivered orders have lower average review scores. I would first split the late-delivery rate by state and product category, then check whether the worst segments also have enough orders to justify action. A high rate in a tiny segment should not automatically take priority.

The next data request would be carrier, promised service level, shipping cost and fulfilment timestamps. These would help separate seller processing delays from transport delays. The current dataset cannot establish that changing a carrier would improve reviews or repeat purchases.

For a pilot, compare a specific operational change with a contemporaneous control group. Track late-delivery rate, review coverage and score, delivery cost and repeat purchases over a fixed observation window. Set the comparison period before reading the result.
